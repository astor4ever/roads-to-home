Roads to Home – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · App: Roads to Home, package pl.grabqa.roadtohome · Developer: GrabQA

Summary

Roads to Home is a logic puzzle game. It does not ask for any personal data and has no accounts. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics (see below) to help us improve the puzzles, and the Android version shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. There are no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (completed levels, best times, stars) and the number of hints in the app's private storage on your device. This data is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To see which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous events to PostHog (PostHog Inc., data stored in the EU, Frankfurt): for example "level started", "level completed" (level number, time, number of hints used), "hint used" and "ad watched", together with the operating system, app version and language. Events are linked to a random identifier generated on your device at first launch – not to your name, e-mail, advertising ID or any other personal identifier. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. Removing the game's data creates a new random identifier.

Ads (Android)

The Android version shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.) only when you choose it: when you run out of hints you can watch a rewarded ad to get one more hint (at most 10 per day). There are no banners and no ads that interrupt the puzzle.

To show and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the device and ad interactions, as an independent controller under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent (Google consent form) before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad consent" button on the main screen. Without consent for personalised ads, Google shows only non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no name or e-mail.
  • No tracking across apps or websites by us.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files access. Permissions used: internet and network state (ads, statistics), advertising ID (ads) and vibration.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.
